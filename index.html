About us

UMBO is a non-government, non-profit organization based in Mangali, Tanudan, Kalinga, established in 2023. It brings the youth and adults of Y-Mangali together through sports.

Objectives

  • Encourage every Y-Mangali youth and adult to take part in sports and physical activity.
  • Promote teamwork and sportsmanship.
  • Foster personal development and self-confidence.
  • Provide opportunities for socialization and fun.
  • Instill discipline, resilience and respect for others.
